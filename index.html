My Story

Background

Yin Chhaychinh grew up in Banteay Meanchey province and completed secondary studies before moving to Phnom Penh to pursue higher education. Numbers and patterns were a constant childhood curiosity — turning everyday puzzles into early lessons in logic.

Why Mathematics

Mathematics offered something rare: a subject where reasoning could be checked, refined, and proven. That precision, paired with the satisfaction of helping classmates finally "get it," shaped a clear decision to pursue both the subject and the craft of teaching it.

Academic Vision

At the Royal University of Phnom Penh, the goal is a strong foundation in pure and applied mathematics — paired with practical experience preparing materials, leading group study sessions, and translating dense ideas into approachable lessons for both high school and university students.

Future Ambitions

The long-term aim is to become a mathematics educator and researcher who builds accessible learning tools — combining classroom teaching with technology, including AI-assisted tutoring resources, to expand access to quality math education across Cambodia.
